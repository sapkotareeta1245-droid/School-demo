Admissions & Student Life

A clear, guided path into the Mohanmaya family.

The Process

Four steps to enrollment

  1. 1

    Submit Online Inquiry or Visit Campus

    Reach out through the form below or come speak with our admissions office directly.

  2. 2

    Entrance Assessment / Interview

    A short assessment helps us place your child at the right level from day one.

  3. 3

    Document Verification

    Bring the required documents for review — see the checklist below.

  4. 4

    Final Enrollment Confirmation

    Once confirmed, your child is officially part of the Mohanmaya family.

Before You Apply

Eligibility & document checklist

Most recent grade sheet or marksheet from the student's previous school, for placement review.

An official transfer certificate from the student's current or most recent school.

A copy of the student's birth certificate for identity and age verification.

Recent passport-sized photographs for the student's school records and ID card.
